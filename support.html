Report a problem with a shot

Tap Why? on the call, scroll down to Something look wrong?, and say what looked wrong. Send report opens an email with what the app saw, for you to look over before you send it.

Which courses can I play?

Any golf course in the United States that's drawn on OpenStreetMap. Bethpage Black comes with the app.

A hole is missing, or drawn wrong

You can draw it yourself on an aerial photo. Tap the hole's name at the top left. Then tap Mark the missing holes, or Redraw hole for the hole you're on.

Can I get a course ready before I play?

Yes. Tap the hole's name at the top left, then Get every hole ready.
